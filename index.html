Sample lesson · Module 05

Understanding M-Pesa & Digital Money

Mobile money turns physical cash into digital value stored safely on your SIM-linked account. Instead of carrying notes in your pocket, you can pay for snacks, send fare, or save shillings directly from your phone.

Every time you deposit cash at an M-Pesa agent, the agent hands you digital shillings called float. Merchant Tills, Paybills, and mobile-linked savings accounts can help you build safe payment habits.

Youth pro tip

Always reconcile your SMS receipts with your weekly ledger. Tracking confirmation codes and merchant fees helps you spot mistakes early.

Term 01

Float

Digital money held by an M-Pesa agent or in your mobile wallet, ready to be converted into cash or sent.

Term 02

Transaction fee

A service charge shown before you confirm certain transfers or withdrawals.

Term 03

Fuliza

An overdraft service that can complete a payment when your balance is short and has fees to understand.
